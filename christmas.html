A Christmas Eve Letter

Juniper the Littlest Reindeer

It’s Christmas Eve at the North Pole, and Juniper, the littlest reindeer, is wondering if she’ll ever have a Christmas job of her own. Then Santa hands her one golden envelope addressed to a very special Little One, and a very important mission: deliver it before morning.

Through dancing northern lights and swirling snow, Juniper finds the courage to fly all the way to a sleeping house, a twinkling tree and a plate of cookies by the fire. The story ends with Santa’s own letter, written to be read aloud.

✦ Good to know

Questions

Who prints and ships the books?

All paperbacks are printed and shipped by Amazon. Delivery dates and the current price are shown on each Amazon page at checkout.

Is the coloring bundle a physical book?

No. It’s a digital download (PDF and PNG files) that you print at home on US Letter paper. Nothing is shipped.

Which book is best for Christmas Eve?

A Christmas Eve Letter was written for that night: it follows Juniper’s delivery and ends with a letter from Santa to read aloud.

Who makes these?

The WEDGE Method is a small independent publisher, WEDGE Method LLC, based in West Jordan, Utah. The Small Wonder Workshop is our hand-painted picture-book series.
